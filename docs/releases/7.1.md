# LUMEN 7.1

- Browser is a new standalone SDK app. Open HTTP or HTTPS sites, follow links,
  page through articles, reload, and move back and forward. It supports static
  HTML, a subset of CSS, and small same-site PNG and JPEG images. Images
  that cannot be displayed fall back to their alternative text. JavaScript is unsupported.
- Home Assistant requests the selected 1-, 6-, or 24-hour history when you open
  an item and handles both ISO and numeric history timestamps.
- Touch feedback keeps the parent control's rounded corners and gives other
  touch targets a minimum radius.
- Reader lookup shows three periods at each end of the first-mention excerpt.
  Tap the excerpt itself to open the longer passage.
- The status bar groups the timer icon and remaining time and reserves space
  for them alongside the app title.
